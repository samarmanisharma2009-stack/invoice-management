Simple invoice management, without the complexity.

Create customers, build invoices, track payments, and manage your billing — all from a lightweight web app backed by Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.

Create an account Log in

Approval-based access

New accounts start as pending. An administrator reviews and approves each request before invoice access is granted.

Customers & invoices

Manage your customer list and create invoices with line items, tax, and discounts — with totals calculated automatically.

Secured by Firestore Rules

Every user's data is isolated at the database level, so no one can read or modify another user's invoices or customers.

How it works

  1. Register for an account with a short verification note.
  2. Wait for an administrator to review and approve your request.
  3. Once approved, log in to create customers and invoices.
  4. Track invoice status: Draft, Pending, Paid, or Cancelled.
